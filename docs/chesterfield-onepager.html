Public-service starting pay falls short of the median rent

  • Starting pay for seven county and school roles is $37,045 to $60,147; every role is short of the median rent of $1,629 by $125 to $703 a month.
  • The best starting salary affords a home near $188,473, about half the median value of $366,000; two police incomes ($120,294) are just enough.

More renters, and more of them are stretched thin

  • Renter households grew by 5,668 in ten years, and cost-burdened renters grew faster; 56% of renters earn 80% of area median income or less.

Housing cost burden is widespread, and heaviest for renters and seniors

How to read these figures

One earner, starting pay. Affordable rent is 30% of gross income; cost burden means 30% or more of income goes to housing. Home price assumes a 7.11% 30-year mortgage (Freddie Mac, week of Nov 12, 2026), 5% down, the county’s proposed FY2027 real estate tax rate ($0.89 per $100), insurance at 0.35% of price and mortgage insurance at 0.7% of the loan (the last two are our assumptions). Hourly pay is annualized at 2,080 hours; school-year workers earn less. Teacher and bus driver pay is the FY26 scale; FY27 pay is proposed, not confirmed adopted. Deputy sheriff pay is from a January 2026 posting; the 911 officer figure is the class-specification minimum. AMI is HUD’s FY2026 area median income for the Richmond area, adjusted for household size. Median rent and home value are 2020-2024 ACS.

Sources: Chesterfield County HR postings (retrieved Nov 23, 2026); CCPS compensation presentation, Feb 3, 2026; U.S. Census Bureau, 2020-2024 American Community Survey 5-year estimates, Tables B25003, B25007, B25009, B25042, B25064, B25070, B25072, B25077, B25091, B25093, B01001, B11001; ACS PUMS incomes against HUD FY2026 income limits; Census Population Estimates Program, Vintages 2019 and 2025.
